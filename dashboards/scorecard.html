Why this sits beside the pipeline, not inside it

The engineered pipeline already validates what it loads. This scorecard exists for a different reader at a different moment: the analyst who owns supplier data quality and needs to know what is wrong with a file the morning it arrives — without opening a repository.

It is deliberately kept out of the Kedro DAG. Wiring it in would make it a duplicate of work the pipeline already does; keeping it separate makes it a different capability, for a different audience.
