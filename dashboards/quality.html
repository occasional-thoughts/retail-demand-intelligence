A profile that changes nothing downstream is an observation, not a deliverable. Every breaching check maps to a decision in the pipeline.

Why this sits beside the pipeline, not inside it

The engineered pipeline validates what it loads. This layer answers a different question for a different reader: the data steward who needs to know what arrived in a file this morning, and who should be able to change a threshold without opening a repository or waiting on a deploy.

That is why the outlier rules are tunable here rather than hard-coded. It is deliberately kept out of the Kedro DAG — wiring it in would make it a duplicate of work the pipeline already does, instead of a separate capability.
